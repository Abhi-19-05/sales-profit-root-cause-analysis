# 📉 Sales Are Growing, Profit Is Under Pressure
## Root Cause Analysis of an E-commerce Business

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end Data Analytics case study conducted on the Brazilian Olist E-commerce dataset. The objective is to investigate why the company's revenue continues to grow while profitability remains under pressure.

Using Python, Pandas, and Power BI, the project covers the complete analytics lifecycle—from business understanding and data preparation to exploratory analysis, root cause analysis, and executive dashboard development.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Management observed the following issue:

> "Revenue is increasing month by month, but profit is not growing at the same pace."

The goal of this project is to identify the key drivers affecting profitability and provide actionable business recommendations.

---

## 📂 Dataset

**Source:** Brazilian E-commerce Public Dataset (Olist)

## 📈 Key Findings

- Revenue showed consistent growth throughout the analysis period.
- Health & Beauty generated the highest revenue.
- Some product categories generated high sales but low profitability.
- Shipping cost significantly impacted profit margins in several categories.
- Certain states experienced much higher shipping costs relative to revenue.
- Shipping percentage and profit margin showed a strong negative relationship.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Optimize logistics and shipping operations in high-cost regions.
- Review pricing strategy for low-margin product categories.
- Increase focus on high-margin product categories.
- Improve inventory planning and warehouse allocation.
- Continuously monitor shipping cost and profit margin KPIs.
